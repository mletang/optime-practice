# Exam-format research — October 7, 2026

This is independent practice for the OpTime CEE Application Essentials preparation context. It is not an official exam or a reproduction of protected test questions.

## Evidence and limits

- Epic-authored [Exam Instructions, updated September 14, 2020](https://it.houstonmethodist.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Exam-Instructions-Examity_091620.pdf), hosted by Houston Methodist: PDF p. 11 shows instructions distinguishing select-one from select-all. Select-all can have one or multiple correct answers. PDF pp. 12–13 describe the testing interface, review of bookmarked/unanswered questions, and automatic submission when time expires. This is historical general guidance; it does not establish a current OpTime CEE blueprint.
- The locally supplied February 2026 With Answers companions contain select-one, select-all, and true/false review questions: examples at OR100 PDF pp. 104 and 122, CLN251/252 pp. 41 and 152, and OR350 p. 329. True/false is included for alignment with the study-material format; its frequency on the actual CEE exam is unverified.
- OR350 PDF pp. 372–373 recommends optional Epic sample exams for learning the online platform and describes access through the training track or Course Catalog. Availability for this particular CEE assessment must be checked in the current track.
- The current signed-in CEE checklist could not initially be reopened because hospital authentication was required. No public primary source established the current OpTime CEE question count, type proportions, topic weighting, pass threshold, or partial-credit rules. The existing 120-minute default remains configurable practice timing; use the current exam cover sheet for authoritative timing and permitted resources.

## Applied to this site

The bank has 120 original questions: 32 fundamentals, 40 configuration, 48 administration. The proportions preserve the previous practice distribution and are not official weighting. Each item has a rationale and companion PDF page references. Single-answer and true/false use radio buttons; select-all uses checkboxes even when only one answer is correct. Prompts disclose selection instructions without disclosing how many answers are correct. True/false choices stay in their natural order; other choices and questions shuffle.

Study mode reveals explanations after checking. Exam mode keeps them hidden until submission, supports revisiting/flagging, and submits at the deadline. Every question is one practice point; select-all requires the exact set. Neither that scoring nor the practice result represents an official passing result. The local timer continues away from the tab; this app does not reproduce Epic's proctor or connectivity-pause behavior.

No exam-dump sites, recalled live test items, or their advertised question counts were used. Source PDFs, extracted training text, credentials, and personal progress are not published.
